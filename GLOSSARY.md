# Glossary

The shared language for Kaushik Kallam's portfolio. Terms describe the portfolio's domain, not how it is built.

## Owner
Kaushik Kallam, the person the portfolio is about and the only authority on its facts, wording and publication.
_Avoid:_ user (ambiguous with visitors), client.

## Visitor
Anyone reading the portfolio. Two kinds matter: the **Recruiter**, who scans for fit, seniority and contact, and the **Research Peer**, who reads for research judgment.
_Avoid:_ user, audience member.

## Journey
The curated, reverse-ordered path through the places the Owner has lived and worked, from New York back to Singapore, ending at Contact. It is a narrative order, not a date sort.
_Avoid:_ flight, timeline, itinerary.

## Chapter
One stop on the Journey, bound to a place and a period of the Owner's life: New York, Texas: career, Silicon Valley, Atlanta, Texas: education, Singapore. The two Texas chapters are always distinct.
_Avoid:_ destination, stop, section.

## Case
A full, dedicated reading page for one substantial piece of work, with its own shareable address. There are five: Data Instrumentation Coverage and Quality, Chegg Mexico, Chegg Discord, Inspire Brands Ad Creative, watched.
_Avoid:_ project page, case study (fine in prose, but "Case" is canonical), post.

## Entry
The short, in-context appearance of a piece of work inside a Chapter or the Selected Work index: its name, the problem, the Owner's contribution and its Status. An Entry may lead to a Case; not every Entry has one.
_Avoid:_ card, teaser, preview.

## Summary
An Entry that expands in place instead of leading to a Case, used where the evidence supports a paragraph but not a full reading page (for example Chase Mobile Entry Points).
_Avoid:_ mini-case.

## Research in Development
Work that is real and current but not finished, shown as a short overview with its maturity stated (bounded multi-agent evaluation). Never presented as a completed Case.
_Avoid:_ upcoming case, coming soon.

## Selected Work
The direct index of Cases and Research in Development, reachable from anywhere without travelling the Journey.
_Avoid:_ projects, portfolio grid.

## Status
The exact, evidence-bounded statement of how far a piece of work went, such as "Dashboard and pipeline created · being publicized" or "Launched project · currently paused". Status is never rounded up.
_Avoid:_ impact, results (unless the evidence supports them).

## Origin
Where the Visitor was when they opened a Case: a specific Entry in a Chapter, or in Selected Work. A Case opened from a shared link or from another Case has no Origin. Return takes the Visitor back to the Origin, with its reading position and focus; without one, Return offers the Case's Chapter.
_Avoid:_ previous page, back state.

## Contribution
What the Owner personally did, as distinct from what the team did. Every Entry and Case states it.
_Avoid:_ ownership, role (a Role is the job title; Contribution is the work).
